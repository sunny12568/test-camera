Pro tips

Look your best on every call

Light your face, not your back

Place a light source in front of you. Avoid bright windows behind you.

Position the camera at eye level

Raise your laptop or webcam so you're looking straight ahead, not up or down.

Check your connection

A stable internet connection ensures smooth, high-quality video during calls.

Keep the lens clean

A quick wipe with a soft cloth can dramatically improve image clarity.

Quick checklist before your call

  • Camera is not covered or physically blocked
  • Browser has camera permission enabled
  • No other app is using the camera
  • You're in a well-lit, quiet environment
  • Background is tidy and professional
